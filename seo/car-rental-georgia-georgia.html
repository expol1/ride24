Traveling around Georgia by car offers great freedom, which is why Ride24 makes it easy to compare offers from local suppliers in one place. Among partner classes, there are offers meeting the criteria of no security deposit, no credit card required, and full insurance, but availability for your dates and insurance details must be checked for each specific offer. Full insurance does not automatically mean the absence of any exclusions or zero deductible.

The Ride24 platform does not operate its own branches, but connects customers with verified local partners. The database of available points includes both airport and city locations. It is worth reviewing pickup options in cities such as Tbilisi, Kutaisi, and Batumi. Some pickup points may involve extra fees or require prior contact, which should always be verified in the details of the selected reservation.

When browsing offers in Georgia, it is worth noting the differences between individual pickup locations. Airport pickup in Tbilisi is available with no extra fee and no contact required. In contrast, for airports in Kutaisi and Batumi, an extra fee applies, and pickup requires contact. In Tbilisi itself, a city pickup location is also available with no extra costs and no contact requirement.

Comparing offers on Ride24 involves reviewing the terms prepared by local partners. Remember that conditions such as no deposit or insurance scope depend on the chosen vehicle class and the specific supplier. Before finalizing your reservation, always check the pickup instructions and exact rental conditions directly in the selected offer.
