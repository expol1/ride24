Ride24 is a car rental brokerage platform connecting you with local partners in Montenegro. When planning your trip, choosing the right pickup location is essential to match your itinerary. In Montenegro, Ride24 collaborates with local partners, providing pickup options across several distinct locations. These include airports as well as city locations, enabling a smooth start to your journey right upon arrival.

Available pickup points include Podgorica Airport (TGD) and Tivat Airport (TIV). For Tivat Airport (TIV), an extra fee of 90 may apply, and contact with the partner is required for both airports. Other available pickup spots are city locations: Budva, Sutomore, and Podgorica City Center. An extra fee of 100 applies in Budva and Sutomore, while in Podgorica City Center the fee is 50. Contact with the partner is required for all these city locations. Specific instructions regarding the vehicle pickup and meeting the partner must be checked directly in the offer details, as Ride24 does not operate its own branches and does not designate exact spots inside terminals or parking zones.

Among Ride24 partner classes in Montenegro, there are offers meeting three core customer expectations: no security deposit, no credit card required, and full insurance. Please keep in mind that full insurance does not automatically mean the absence of all exclusions or zero deductible. The availability of this specific combination for your chosen dates, along with the precise coverage scope, must always be verified within the offer conditions.

Using the Ride24 platform allows you to conveniently compare offers provided by local partners. The comparison tool helps you evaluate different vehicle classes, potential extra fees associated with specific locations, and rental conditions. Before finalizing your booking, always review the up-to-date terms regarding your selected car and pickup point.
