Planning a trip and need a car? Ride24 is a car rental brokerage platform cooperating with local partners in the Calabria region, Italy.

Traveling across the Calabria region gives you the freedom to discover scenic places in Italy at your own pace. Through the Ride24 platform, you can compare proposals prepared by local partners and choose the vehicle that best suits your travel plans. Please keep in mind that Ride24 does not operate its own rental desks – we act as a brokerage platform facilitating the comparison of offers and finding suitable transport.

In this location, travelers have access to different pickup points that differ in their specifics. Available locations include Lamezia Terme Airport (SUF) as well as Lamezia Terme Centrale Railway Station. Please note that for Lamezia Terme Centrale Railway Station, contacting the partner is required. It is worth paying attention to these differences when planning your travel logistics to choose the pickup spot that best fits your arrival arrangements.

Regarding financial and insurance conditions, partner classes in the Calabria region feature declarations meeting three key criteria sought after by customers: no security deposit, no credit card required, and full insurance. This means such options do exist among partner offers, but their actual availability for your chosen dates and the precise scope of coverage must always be verified directly against the specific offer. Full insurance does not automatically mean the absence of all exclusions or zero excess – detailed insurance terms and potential exclusions should always be checked in the individual offer.

The process of using the platform is straightforward and clear. To find a suitable vehicle, use our search engine where you can compare available offers from local partners. Carefully review the rental terms, pickup instructions, and insurance rules for the selected car. This will help you avoid surprises and smoothly collect your keys upon arrival in the Calabria region.
