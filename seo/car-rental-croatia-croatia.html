In Croatia, vehicle pickup points are located in various areas, allowing you to choose a convenient location based on your travel itinerary. Available locations include airport points such as Zagreb airport, Pula airport, Zadar airport, Dubrovnik airport, and Split airport, which allow you to pick up a car right after arrival. Alternatively, city and port locations include Pula downtown, Rabac downtown, Zadar downtown, Zadar port, Biograd na moru Downtown, Primošten downtown, and Šibenik downtown.

Individual locations differ in character and offer availability depending on the chosen partner. Airport points are typically close to arrival terminals, while downtown or port locations may be situated in different parts of the urban infrastructure. The exact meeting place, key handover method, and pickup instructions should always be checked in the details of your selected reservation.

Ride24 does not operate its own fleet branches but acts as an intermediary platform. This means the booking process relies on offers provided by external local partners. Comparing offers on the website allows you to review available vehicle classes and preview the rental conditions.

Before confirming your reservation, remember to carefully read the terms and conditions of the chosen offer. Check whether the selected car meets your expectations regarding equipment, fuel policy, and payment or security requirements.
