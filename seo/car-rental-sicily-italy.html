Sicily is a large region where renting a car makes getting around much easier. By using Ride24.pl, you do not book a car directly with us – our platform acts as an intermediary for booking vehicles with trusted local partners. Among the partner classes on this island, there are offers that simultaneously meet the three criteria customers look for: no security deposit, no credit card required, and full insurance. However, keep in mind that availability for specific dates and the exact scope of protection must always be checked in the details of the chosen offer.

Sicily offers several diverse pickup points, which differ in their location and service conditions. Seaports, such as Trapani Port and Catania Port, allow you to collect a car in coastal areas; for Catania Port, the source indicates an additional fee of 10 and a contact requirement. Airports, including Trapani Airport (TPS), Palermo Airport (PMO), and Catania Airport (CTA), serve as convenient hubs upon arrival. At Catania Airport, the additional fee is 0, and contact is not required. Additionally, pickup options are available at a hotel (Catania - Hotel with an additional fee of 20 and a contact requirement) and an office (Castellammare del Golfo - Office, where there is no extra fee, but contact is required).

Regardless of whether you choose a seaport, airport, hotel, or office, the exact method of handing over the keys and meeting instructions with the partner can be found in your booking documentation. Due to the variety of locations and the specifics of local providers, always carefully verify the rules applicable to your chosen pickup point. Full insurance does not automatically mean the absence of all exclusions or zero deductible, so it is important to review the terms of the agreement.

Comparing offers on Ride24.pl allows you to review proposals from local partners and choose the option that best fits your travel itinerary. When searching for a car, keep your priorities in mind, such as no security deposit or no credit card requirement.
