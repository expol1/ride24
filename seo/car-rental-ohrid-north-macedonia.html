Among the partner classes in the Ohrid region, there are offers that simultaneously meet the three key criteria sought by Ride24 customers: no security deposit, no credit card required, and full insurance. However, keep in mind that exact availability for your chosen dates and the precise scope of insurance protection always need to be verified for each specific vehicle, and full insurance does not automatically mean the absence of all exclusions or zero excess.

Ride24 is an intermediary platform that facilitates car reservations with trusted local providers. We do not operate our own branches or vehicle fleets, but we help you compare proposals and choose the optimal means of transport tailored to your travel itinerary.

In the Ohrid region, you can choose from several distinct pick-up points, which differ in their location. The first is Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport (OHD), convenient for those arriving by air. The second option is a city location at Partizanska 1, and the third is an office located in Peštani at Naum Ohridski 89. Each of these locations allows you to start your journey from a convenient spot.

Before picking up the car, make sure to carefully review the pick-up instructions provided by the partner. Always check the rental terms for the selected car, including the exact rules regarding insurance and any procedural requirements.
