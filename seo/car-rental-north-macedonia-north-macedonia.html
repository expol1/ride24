Traveling across North Macedonia by car gives you the freedom to explore scenic lake regions and vibrant cities at your own pace. As an intermediary platform, Ride24.pl brings together proposals from local rental suppliers in one place, allowing you to conveniently compare conditions and prices before making a decision.

Among the partner classes, there are offers meeting key customer criteria: no security deposit, no credit card required, and full insurance. However, please remember that availability for specific dates and the exact scope of protection must always be checked in the details of the selected offer. Full insurance does not automatically mean the absence of all exclusions or zero excess, so we always recommend reviewing the rental agreement terms carefully.

We offer a variety of pick-up points across the country to suit your itinerary. You can collect a vehicle at airports such as Skopje International Airport (SKP) and Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport (OHD). When choosing Skopje International Airport (SKP), an extra fee of 40 applies, whereas at Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport (OHD) there is no extra fee. Additionally, city and office locations are available, including Ohrid - Partizanska 1 and Peštani - Naum Ohridski 89, both of which also involve no extra fees.

Individual locations vary in character – from major airport hubs to points situated in cities and smaller towns. Choosing the right spot depends on your travel itinerary and destination. Before finalizing your booking, make sure to read the pick-up instructions and conditions applicable to your chosen partner. Ride24.pl does not operate its own branches, meaning all vehicle handover procedures and service execution are handled directly by the local supplier.

Using our search engine, you can easily filter available vehicles and tailor them to your needs. Compare offers, check insurance terms, and review any payment requirements. Book your car with Ride24.pl and hit the road in North Macedonia on your own terms.
